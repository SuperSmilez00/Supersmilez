Good to know

Questions, answered.

How long does a session take?

About an hour. The whitening light runs for three rounds of 15 minutes, and the rest is getting set up and checking your results.

Does it hurt?

No. The treatment is pain-free. You lie back in the recliner and relax while the light works.

Can I eat and drink normally afterwards?

Follow the white diet for at least 24 hours: white or clear foods and drinks only, such as yogurt, rice, chicken, fish, milk and white bread. Keep brushing twice daily and flossing every day.
